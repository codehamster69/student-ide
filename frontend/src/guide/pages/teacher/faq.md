## Can I fail a student because of a moment?

No. A moment is a reason for a conversation. It never changes marks, attendance or a competition place, and the screen says so. Ask the student, using the suggested questions, and remember habits change for ordinary reasons.

## Why is a session "Not scored yet"?

The student needs at least two baseline sessions (in their first 28 days). Competition sessions need at least two other competitions.

## Can I delete a lab?

Not once students have recorded in it. Close it instead. Drafts without recordings can be deleted.

## Why can't I edit or remove a question?

A question can be deleted only while nobody has recorded on it. You can still edit it; old recordings keep the copy the student saw.

## Does View as student create study data?

No. These sessions are marked as previews and are excluded from baselines, review, live view, attendance and exports.

## A student says their attendance is wrong.

Open the lab's **Attendance** page, check the reason, tap the mark to override and send. See [Attendance](attendance).

## Do students know when I watch live?

Always. Their editor shows your name while you view it, and a pill while the class view is open. See [Live view](live-view).

## A Sandbox Lab environment is stuck at Queued.

Press **Build now** on the version. If it failed, **Try again**. The build runs off the main server and can take several minutes.

## Why did a student's Sandbox submission get refused?

The lab is closed or past due (plus a short grace) under your late policy. Their work is still saved. Change the policy in the wizard's Publish step if needed.

## Who can see my labs?

You, co-teachers you add, and admins.
