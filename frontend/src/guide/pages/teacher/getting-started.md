## Sign in

Open the teacher area and sign in with your college Google account. Your role comes from your address:

- `ug…` / `pg…` addresses (for example `pg25cs07@…`) are **students**.
- Any other college address (`firstname.lastname@…`) is a **teacher**.
- Addresses in the server's admin list are **admins**: teachers who can also open **People** and change anyone's role. TAs are usually made teachers here.

In a local dev setup there is no Google sign-in; you enter the research token instead.

## First-setup checklist

**Getting started** (open it from the header) lists these steps and ticks them as you go:

1. **Set up Roll Call** (optional, needed for attendance). One press makes your own register. See [Attendance](attendance).
2. **Create a lab.** A lab is one sitting for one class. Give it a subject. See [Making a lab](making-a-lab).
3. **Add questions.** Write your own or pick from the bundled sets and platforms. See [Question sources](question-sources).
4. **Attach class groups** from Roll Call so their students join the roster.
5. **Try it as a student** with View as student.
6. **Run the class, then close the lab.** Open the lab, watch it live, and closing it sends attendance.

## Where things are

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Labs** | Make and run labs and competitions |
| **Subjects** | Labs filed by course, with their class groups |
| **Live** | Watch an open lab as it runs |
| **Review** | Timelines of recorded sessions after class |
| **Sandbox** | Sandbox Labs: subjects, environments, library |

## View as student

**View as student** in the header opens the student side exactly as students see it: your own and shared labs (drafts are marked), the learning lab and practice tasks. Run, Check, Reset, tabs and resume all work.

Nothing you do there is study data. These sessions are marked as previews and never reach baselines, the detector, review, live view, lab progress, attendance, or the research export. There is no consent screen for staff. Competitions cannot be joined from this view, so staff never appear on a class board. Use **Back to Teach** in the bar at the bottom to leave.

## One principle to keep in mind

The review screens show moments worth a conversation. They are a reason to talk to a student, never a verdict. See [Review timeline](review-timeline).
