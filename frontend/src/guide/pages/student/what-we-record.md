Student IDE is part of a research study on *how* people write code. This page explains the consent screen in plain words. The consent screen itself is the text you agree to.

## What is recorded

While you work in the editor it records:

- When each key is pressed and released (the timing, and which key).
- Every edit to your code.
- Anything you paste, copy or cut in the editor.
- Each time you run your program, with its output.
- When you switch away from the editor or the browser tab.

In a [Sandbox Lab](sandbox-labs) it also records the lab's terminal: keys, commands you run, pastes into the terminal, files that change outside the editor, packages you install, and a copy of your lab folder so your work follows you to another computer.

The **Recording** pill at the top of the editor tells you it is on. If the connection drops it says **Offline** and counts the events waiting; they are sent when you are back online.

## What is not recorded

No webcam, no microphone, no screen recording, and nothing from other apps or windows. In a Sandbox Lab the app sees only the lab's own folder and container, not your other files.

## What it is used for

Your typing patterns are compared only with **your own** earlier sessions, never with other students. If part of a session looks different from how you usually write, a teacher may see it as a *moment worth a conversation*. That is all it ever leads to: a conversation. It never changes your marks, your attendance or your place on a competition board, and it is never an automatic penalty.

## How long it is kept

Raw keystroke data is deleted once summary statistics have been computed: when your four-week baseline is built, and never kept longer than 28 days. The summaries stay for the study.

## Who can see it

Your teachers (and admins) can look at a session's timeline and replay your code for labs they run. During a lab your teacher may also follow the class live. If they open a read-only view of your editor, the editor tells you with a notice that names them. See [the FAQ](faq).

## Practice and previews

Learning-lab practice is recorded as extra typing data for the study but kept apart: it is never compared with your lab work, never shown to teachers and never marked. The Recording pill says so when you hover it.
