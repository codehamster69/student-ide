## Will a flag get me in trouble?

No. A flag, called a *moment worth a conversation*, only ever leads to a conversation with your instructor. It is never an automatic penalty, never changes marks or attendance and never affects a competition place. After such a conversation your instructor may note how it went (for example "explained, own work"). Only staff see this note, it is never a mark, and the research reports only class-wide counts without names.

## Can I paste code?

Yes, your own. In labs and competitions you can copy, cut and paste anything inside Scribelab: your code, code from another question of the same lab, and text from the question. Pasting text copied from somewhere else (another website, an AI chat, another app) is turned off, and a short notice says so. Type it yourself instead.

Pastes, and attempts to paste from outside, are recorded like everything else. Pastes are not counted as typing for attendance. The learning lab is practice, so pasting works there as usual.

## A notice says my teacher is viewing my editor.

During a lab your teacher can open a read-only copy of your editor. The editor always tells you, naming them, and the notice ends when they leave. They cannot type in your editor. A small **Class view on** pill means they have the class overview open, which shows who is working and your runs and checks, not your code. Nothing seen live is used to mark your work.

## The pill says Offline.

Your edits are waiting to be sent and are sent when the connection returns. Keep working; nothing is lost.

## Did I lose my work if I closed the tab?

Everything is saved as you work. Open the lab again with **Continue lab** and each question picks up where you left it.

## I changed my mind about a question. How do I start again?

Press **Reset** above the editor. Press `Ctrl + Z` right after if you did not mean it.

## Why can't I see a lab?

The lab may be a draft, closed, or not assigned to your roll number. Ask your teacher.

## My Sandbox Lab will not open.

The error shows the failing setup checks with a fix for each. Common ones are virtualization turned off, low disk space, or needing a restart after WSL2 is enabled. See [Sandbox Labs](sandbox-labs).

## I think an attendance mark is wrong.

Tell your teacher. They can override a mark before it is sent. See [Attendance](attendance).

## Who do I ask about the study?

Your instructor. See [What we record](what-we-record) for what is collected.
