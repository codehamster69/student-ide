The learning lab is open to you any time. No roster, no lab time and no teacher needed. Open it from the **Learning lab** card on your home page.

## Tracks

Lessons are grouped into tracks, from beginner to advanced:

- **C**: Beginner and Advanced
- **C++**: Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced
- **Python**: Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced

A track opens like a lab: one editor with a tab per lesson, Previous and Next, and the lesson text in the side panel.

## A lesson

1. Read the explanation in the side panel.
2. Press **Try it** on any code example to put it in the editor, then change it and **Run**. `Ctrl + Z` brings your own code back.
3. Do the small exercise at the end of the lesson and press **Check**.

A lesson is **done** once Check passes (or, for a lesson without tests, once you have run code in it). Opening a lesson again brings back your last code. Your progress shows on the learning lab page.

Press **All lessons** in the top bar to go back to the list.

## What is recorded

Practice is recorded like a lab, because typing is useful data for the study. It is kept apart: it is never compared with your lab work, never shown to teachers and never marked. Raw keystrokes are deleted on the same schedule as everything else. See [What we record](what-we-record).
