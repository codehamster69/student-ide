Some labs mark attendance automatically in **Roll Call**, the attendance app your teachers use. You do not tap anything.

## The rule

For each lab day you count as **present** when you have both:

- at least **10 active minutes**: minutes in which you typed, ran or checked code, and
- at least **40 typed characters**: text you actually typed. Pastes, drops and Reset do not count.

Your teacher can change these numbers for a lab, and can correct anyone's mark before it is sent, so ask them if you think a mark is wrong.

In a Sandbox Lab the terminal counts too: printable keys typed in the terminal or in vim or nano count as typed characters, and running a command, Run, Check or saving a file from the terminal counts as an active minute.

## What does not count

Learning-lab practice and teacher previews never count. And attendance never looks at the review timeline: a highlighted moment does not change whether you are present.

## My attendance

When one of your labs belongs to a class group, **My attendance** appears in the header. It opens Roll Call signed in as you, and shows your own marks (read-only), grouped by subject. You can see only your own marks.

Attendance for a lab day is sent when your teacher closes the lab or moves on to the next group, so marks can take until then to appear.

## Roll number

Roll Call matches you by your college address. Your roll number must match the roster your teacher set up (for example `UG23CS001`). If you are missing from a register, tell your teacher.
