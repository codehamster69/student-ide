## What happened

Rohan (2026-11-25): "our detector even flagged copying and pasting from our own ide which should
not be, and we should just not allow pasting copied content from other pages." The pilot showed
students' own copy and paste (moving a block, duplicating a line, reusing code from the previous
question) counted as paste evidence (`paste_frac`, Review paste markers, Live paste count).

Added a paste guard (`frontend/src/pasteguard.ts`, `capture.ts` option `guard`):

- On study screens (lab questions, competitions, Sandbox Labs editor and desktop terminal; also
  View as student so staff see what students see) every copy, cut or drag start on the page is
  remembered as a 53-bit hash of the normalised text (CRLF, nbsp, trailing spaces), 24 h in
  localStorage, so it works across question tabs of a sitting, a reload and other tabs.
- A paste or drop is allowed when its text matches a remembered copy (`source` "editor" or
  "page") or already occurs in one of the student's open documents ("document"). Otherwise it is
  blocked, a notice says "Pasting from outside Scribelab is turned off here. Copy and paste within
  your own code still works.", and a `clipboard` event `{blocked: true, source: "external"}` keeps
  the text the student tried to paste. No edit follows.
- Allowed pastes and drops get edit origin `input.paste.internal` / `input.drop.internal`.
  Review (paste list, timeline: blocked = outlined diamond), Live paste count, attendance
  typed/pasted counts and the similarity check's paste stats now treat them as ordinary editing.
  The detector change is done in the pilot re-tune thread (features.py), which also classifies
  past pilot pastes as internal by matching earlier in-editor copies.
- Schema v4 (additive): `clipboard.action` "drop", `source`, `blocked`, `surface`.

## Why

- Blocking rather than only flagging: Rohan's call. It removes the cheapest copying route (paste
  an LLM answer) and leaves the detector to watch typing, which is the thesis's actual signal.
- Match on what was copied inside, not on what is in the clipboard: browsers do not let a page
  know where clipboard text came from. Remembering in-app copies (hash only, no text stored
  outside the event log) is the only reliable "inside" test; the own-document fallback covers a
  lost list (private mode, a copy older than 24 h).
- Copies out of form fields (stdin box) never count as inside, so text pasted there cannot be
  laundered into the editor. Copies on exempt screens (learning lab) are not remembered either.
- Learning lab exempt: it is practice, not study data (context "learn"), and lessons invite
  trying examples. Teacher authoring editors never had capture.
- Blocked attempts are logged because an attempt to paste outside text is itself a copying
  signal for the thesis (RQ on copying), and the attempted text shows what was being copied.
- Consent text unchanged: it already says "anything you paste, copy or cut in the editor" is
  recorded. The student guide (What we record, FAQ "Can I paste code?") now says outside pastes
  are turned off and attempts are recorded.

## Follow-ups

- Limits to state in the thesis: text inserted by a browser extension, IME or autofill is not a
  paste event and is seen as typing (the detector still sees its timing); a determined student
  can retype from a second screen, which is what the identity and process channels are for.
- Count blocked-paste attempts per session as a pilot statistic once a few weeks of v4 data exist.
