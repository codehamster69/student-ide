## What happened

Agreed build order:

1. **Event-logging editor** (the Student IDE itself).
2. **Feature extraction and CUSUM detector,** developed against a
   synthetic-data pipeline.
3. **Teacher timeline dashboard.**
4. **Evaluation harness:** staged sessions, baselines, fairness, and
   precision at realistic cheating rates.

## Why

- The logger comes first because Phase 1 baseline labs (months 3–4) need a
  working, ethics-approved recorder, and every later stage depends on its
  event format.
- The detector is built on **synthetic sessions** (e.g. splicing two
  students' event streams at a known minute) so it can be developed and
  tested before real Phase 2 data exists, and so the ground-truth switch
  time is known exactly.
- The dashboard comes after the detector because its job is to show the
  detector's evidence; designing it earlier would mean guessing at what it
  displays.
- The evaluation harness comes last but is designed for RQ4 from the
  start: precision at 1/100 and 1/500 prevalence and per-subgroup error
  rates, not just overall accuracy.
